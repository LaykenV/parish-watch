Public Parish landing experiments

Three directions from docs/marketing-page.md. Same exact copy, same four-part structure, same palette. Different typography, composition, and visual signature. Test each at 320, 375, 414, 768, and a desktop width.

  • A — The Docket

    Editorial record. Evidence tie-lines connect the hero's claims to anchor plates. The proof is an auto-advancing record sheet with step controls.

    Newsreader · Public Sans · Spline Sans Mono

  • B — Field Notes

    Surveyor's traverse. Fanned field sheets in the hero; a dashed traverse line with four stations carries the evidence sequence on scroll.

    Fraunces · Atkinson Hyperlegible · IBM Plex Mono

  • C — Record Room

    Dark inspection room. Gold brackets frame the thesis and the specimen sheet. On mobile the steps become a native snap rail with the next card peeking.

    Bricolage Grotesque · Instrument Sans · JetBrains Mono

Prototype quality. Fonts load from Google Fonts for comparison only; production would self-host. See experiments/landing/README.md for the handoff.
